Munnar

Rolling tea gardens, cool misty mornings, and mountain views that stay with you long after you leave.

Idukki, Kerala
4.8 Rating
Best: Sep – Mar
ABOUT THE PLACE

Why Visit Munnar

Munnar sits nearly 1,600 metres above sea level in the Western Ghats, where three mountain streams meet among endless carpets of tea. Once the summer retreat of British planters, it has kept its cool climate and unhurried pace while becoming one of South India's most loved hill stations.

Mornings here begin in mist, with tea pickers moving between manicured rows and the Anamudi peak, the highest point in South India, watching over it all. Beyond the plantations, Munnar opens into spice gardens, waterfalls, and the rare Neelakurinji blooms that colour the hills blue once every twelve years.

THINGS TO DO

Top Attractions

Tea Gardens

Walk through the plantations and visit the Tea Museum to see how the leaf is made.

Eravikulam Park

Home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr and sweeping views of the Rajamala hills.

Trekking Trails

Guided treks through Kolukkumalai for a sunrise above the clouds.

Photo Points

Top Station and Echo Point offer some of Kerala's most photographed views.

Best Time to Visit
  • September to March — pleasant, cool weather, ideal for sightseeing
  • Avoid heavy monsoon months (June–Aug) for outdoor treks
  • Carry light woollens, evenings can drop below 15°C
How to Reach
  • Nearest airport: Kochi (110 km, ~3 hrs by road)
  • Nearest railway: Aluva / Ernakulam
  • Well connected by road from Kochi and Madurai
