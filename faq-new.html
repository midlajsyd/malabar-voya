Questions, answered

Everything you may want to know before planning a trip with Malabar Voyages.

How do I book a trip?

Choose a package on the Packages page, then fill in the booking form. Our team will contact you to confirm the details.

Can I change a package to suit my plans?

Yes. Our trips are handcrafted, so tell us your dates, group size and interests in the Contact form and we will adjust the plan.

Which destinations do you cover?

We plan trips across Kerala, including Munnar and Alleppey, other parts of India, and international destinations such as Bali.

What is included in a package price?

Each package card on the Packages page lists its price and trip duration. Check the package details for what is included.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Please contact us as early as possible with your booking details and we will explain the options available.

Is the website available on mobile?

Yes. The site is responsive, so it works on phones, tablets and desktops.
